<h1>Accessible Intelligence</h1>

<h3>
  Making AI-generated intelligence accessible,
  understandable and actionable for diverse people.
</h3>

<p>Accessible Intelligence is an integrative perspective that connects research in accessibility, human-centred AI, explainable AI and responsible AI. It foregrounds whether people with diverse abilities, knowledge, languages and circumstances can meaningfully access, interpret, evaluate, use and influence AI-generated intelligence.</p>

<p style="font-size: 0.875rem; color: #666;">
  This perspective is developed through the research and writing of
  <strong>Fotios Spyridonis</strong>, Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Computer Science
  at Brunel University of London.
</p>

<h2 id="why">Why Accessible Intelligence</h2>

<p>AI can remove longstanding accessibility barriers while introducing new ones. An AI system may provide an accessible interface but produce explanations that are difficult to interpret. An intelligent assistant may reduce physical interaction demands while introducing new challenges for supervision and intervention. A recommendation may be clearly presented without giving someone sufficient information or opportunity to question it.

**Accessible interfaces do not necessarily make AI-generated intelligence accessible.**

Research in accessible computing, human-centred AI, explainable AI and responsible AI already addresses important aspects of these challenges. Accessible Intelligence connects these contributions by considering how accessibility affects meaningful participation throughout AI-mediated interaction.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>👤 Human Agency</h3>

<p>Enabling people to question, correct, redirect or challenge AI behaviour through accessible mechanisms.</p>

<h2>Accessible Intelligence across computing</h2>

<div class="card-grid">

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Generative AI</h3>

<p>Accessible and contextually useful AI-generated content, with opportunities to identify and correct omissions.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Explainable AI</h3>

<p>Explanations that support meaningful understanding and judgement for people with diverse abilities.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>AI Agents</h3>

<p>Accessible supervision, intervention and control as intelligent systems act increasingly autonomously.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Responsible AI</h3>

<p>Ensuring safeguards such as transparency, oversight and contestability can be meaningfully accessed and exercised.</p>
