<h1>Accessible Intelligence</h1>

<h3>Making AI-generated intelligence accessible, understandable and actionable for diverse people.</h3>

<p>Accessible Intelligence is an integrative perspective that connects research in accessibility, human-centred AI, explainable AI and responsible AI. It foregrounds whether people with diverse abilities, knowledge, languages and circumstances can meaningfully access, interpret, evaluate, use and influence AI-generated intelligence.</p>

<p style="font-size: 0.875rem; color: #666;">
  This perspective is developed through the research and writing of
  <strong>Fotios Spyridonis</strong>, Senior Lecturer (Associate Professor) in Computer Science
  at Brunel University of London.
</p>

<h2 id="why">Why Accessible Intelligence</h2>

<p>As artificial intelligence increasingly generates explanations, recommendations, predictions and decisions, accessibility must concern more than the interfaces through which people interact with these systems. Accessible Intelligence foregrounds whether people with diverse abilities, knowledge, languages and circumstances can meaningfully access, interpret, evaluate, use and influence AI-generated intelligence. </p>

<p>The perspective builds upon established scholarship rather than proposing a new framework or research field. Its purpose is to connect accessibility concerns across AI interaction, generated information, human judgement and agency.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h2>Accessible Intelligence across computing</h2>

<div class="card-grid">

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Generative AI</h3>

<p>Accessible and contextually useful AI-generated content, with opportunities to identify and correct omissions.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Explainable AI</h3>

<p>Explanations that support meaningful understanding and judgement for people with diverse abilities.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>AI Agents</h3>

<p>Accessible supervision, intervention and control as intelligent systems act increasingly autonomously.</p>

</div>

<div class="research-card">

<h3>Responsible AI</h3>
